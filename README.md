# Splatbot: Senior Capstone Project
#### Ben Webster
Last updated 10/29/2026

## Background
Taking 3D scans of rooms is a cumbersome process. Pictures must be taken from many angles and positions to create a high-fidelity scan. I aim to make the process easy and enjoyable by designing a small robot capable of navigating a room while capturing optimal images for a 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) reconstruction.

## Objectives

### Must-have Goals:
* A custom-built vehicle equipped with a camera capable of being manually piloted around a room.
* Camera output feeds to an external device, such as a nearby laptop, that uses COLMAP for Structure from Motion (SfM) and 3DGS.
* The robot streams a lower resolution livestream to the controlling device as it traverses the room.
* Autonomously navigates around an enclosed room during data capture.
